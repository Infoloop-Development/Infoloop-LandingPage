    Solid Loop Orange and Ink squares (800, 1200 px). Place employee headshot
    cutouts on these. Pair by contrast: ink backdrop with the light banner;
    never a black circle on the dark banner.

    1920x1080 backgrounds for Zoom / Meet / Teams, dark and light. Lockup
    top-left, tagline bottom-right, mark texture at the edges - the center
    stays clear for the person. Use the dark one by default; it reads best
    on camera and hides edge artifacts from background replacement.

CORE RULES (the short version)
------------------------------
Colors   Ink #0A0A0A, Loop Orange #F47B00, White #FFFFFF. 90% ink/white,
         10% orange. Orange pairs with ink, never carries body text.
Fonts    DM Sans (display + wordmark), Open Sans (text). Both free on
         Google Fonts:
         fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans
         fonts.google.com/specimen/Open+Sans
Name     Always lowercase: infoloop. "Infoloop Technologies Inc." in legal
         documents only. Never InfoLoop, Info Loop, INFOLOOP or info-loop.
Mark     Five bars, fixed proportions, accent always on bar 2. Never redraw
         by eye - export from the masters in this kit.
Writing  Plain words. No em dashes. Claims with numbers behind them.

Questions about usage: see the guidelines PDF, or ask the brand owner.
