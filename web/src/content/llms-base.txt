# Infoloop

> Infoloop is a US software development company that designs, builds and runs custom applications, AI automation and Webflow and Shopify websites for manufacturing, healthcare, SaaS and biorenewables businesses. Tagline: We build. We run.

## What Infoloop does
- Custom applications, enterprise applications, eCommerce and digital storefronts, no-code and low-code solutions, custom web development, SaaS product development (Build)
- UX optimization and accessibility, IT strategy and process optimization, application maintenance and support, IT staff augmentation (Grow)
- Legacy app modernization, AI and advanced tech solutions (Transform)
- Product, tech and design consulting (Consulting)
- In-house products: OpsDeck (attendance management for manufacturing), GarageZone (garage management), LoopIQ (learning and testing platform), Verko (AI governance and compliance)

## How Infoloop works
- 30-minute discovery call, then scope in a week with a fixed price in writing
- Most projects live in 4 to 8 weeks, with weekly demos on real data
- After launch Infoloop monitors, fixes and improves the software every month and sends a short report ("We run")
- Clients own the code, accounts, domains and data from day one

## Proof
- 4.8 average client rating (Trustpilot 4.9, Google 4.8, Clutch 4.7, GoodFirms 4.7)
- 50+ projects delivered, software running in 6 countries, 99.9% uptime on software Infoloop runs
- Certified Webflow and Shopify Partners
